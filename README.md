# AtlasAnalytics

Payments analytics and decision-intelligence portfolio built around the AtlasPay domain.

AtlasAnalytics is designed for Data Analyst, Analytics Engineer, and Data Scientist hiring evidence. It focuses on trustworthy metric definitions, dimensional modeling, advanced SQL, reproducible synthetic data, data-quality checks, Python analysis, and decision-oriented reporting.

## Principles

- Synthetic data is labeled synthetic and generated reproducibly.
- Every metric has a grain, business definition, source, caveat, and decision use.
- SQL models are testable and executable, not screenshots or notebook-only logic.
- Analytics should explain operational and business decisions, not just visualize numbers.
- No production-volume, production-traffic, or real-customer claims are made without evidence.

## Initial roadmap

1. Payments warehouse foundation with dimensions and lifecycle facts.
2. Authorization, timeout, reversal, decline, and reconciliation marts.
3. Advanced SQL analysis with cohorts, percentiles, rolling baselines, and anomaly signals.
4. Python/Polars analysis and reproducible decision reports.
5. Executive and operations dashboards.
6. Risk/fraud monitoring with leakage-safe temporal evaluation and expected-cost thresholds.
